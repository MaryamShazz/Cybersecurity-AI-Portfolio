# 🎓 Final Course Summary

Completing **Huawei HCIA-Security V4.0** provided me with a structured introduction to enterprise network security and Huawei's firewall technologies. The course progresses from fundamental security concepts to practical enterprise technologies, covering firewall deployment, NAT, high availability, intrusion prevention, authentication, cryptography, PKI, and VPN solutions.

I completed the course by working through each module, watching the training videos, completing the associated assessments, and documenting my learning throughout this repository. The practical exercises included in the training are documented separately within each module and are clearly identified as **course labs** rather than independent implementations.

I originally began the course in **December 2025**, completed most of the material, then paused to focus on several personal cybersecurity projects before returning to finish the remaining modules. Revisiting the course after that break also reinforced many of the earlier networking and security concepts before moving into the advanced topics.

---

## 🌟 Highlights

Several topics stood out as particularly valuable during the course:

### ⚙️ Firewall High Availability

Learning how **VRRP**, **HRP**, and **VGMP** work together to provide firewall redundancy gave me a much clearer understanding of enterprise high-availability design. Seeing how these protocols interact to maintain service continuity helped connect concepts that had previously seemed unrelated.

### 🔐 VPN Technologies

The comparison between **IPSec VPN** and **SSL VPN** was another highlight. Understanding the differences between site-to-site and remote-access VPNs—and the trade-offs between security, configuration complexity, and deployment scenarios—provided useful context for secure network design.

### 🛡️ Enterprise Firewall Security

The modules covering firewall security policies, NAT, IPS, and AAA demonstrated how multiple security mechanisms work together to protect enterprise networks rather than functioning as isolated features.
