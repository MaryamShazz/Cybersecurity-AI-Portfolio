# What I Learned in Module 01

- Network security covers protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data across a network, from the physical layer up through the applications running on top of it.
- Networks weren't originally designed with security in mind; as they opened up and connected to the internet, security had to become its own discipline rather than an afterthought.
- Common threats break down into attacks on confidentiality (eavesdropping, data theft), integrity (tampering, man in the middle), and availability (DoS/DDoS), plus social engineering and credential based attacks.
- The industry is shifting from a pure "perimeter defense" model toward zero trust and continuous verification.
- **ISO 27001** defines an Information Security Management System (ISMS), a management framework built around risk assessment, defined controls, and continuous improvement, rather than a fixed technical checklist.
- **China's Cybersecurity Classified Protection System** grades information systems by the level of protection required, based on potential impact of a breach, with required controls scaling to the grade.
